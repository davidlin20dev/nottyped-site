What you can do

Handwritten only

There’s no keyboard. Write a note one stroke at a time and share it in your own hand.

Any pen. Even your finger.

Finger, Apple Pencil, or any stylus. If it can touch the screen, it can write.

Comment by hand

Reply to a note the same way it was made: handwritten, never typed.

Follow writers

Find people whose handwriting you love and follow them to fill your feed.

Dedicate a note

Write one for someone. It carries their name at the top, and we let them know.

Share it anywhere

Turn any note into a card, or a video of it being written, and post it to Instagram, Threads, or wherever your people are.
