Version history

Every NotTyped release, newest first.

  1. 1.8.0 Latest

    Nov 26, 2026

    Pencil sound: hear a real pencil on paper as you write. It follows your hand, louder when you write faster and quiet when you pause. Turn it off in Settings > Writing. Silent mode turns it off too.

  2. 1.7.0

    Nov 21, 2026
    • Made for iPhone Duo: NotTyped fills the big screen and turns any way you hold it.
    • On iPad with iPadOS 27, resize NotTyped's window freely. Writing fits even a short one.
    • Notes written with Apple Pencil (USB-C) go through now.
    • Small fixes for iOS 27.

  3. 1.6.0

    Nov 14, 2026
    • Invite a friend with a card in your own handwriting: write a line, and it goes out with a link to NotTyped.
    • Write from anywhere: say “Write in NotTyped” to Siri, or add a Write a note button to Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button.

  4. 1.5.0

    Nov 7, 2026
    • Notification settings now work: switch off comments, follows, follow requests, dedications or sends, and those notifications stop on every device.
    • Remind me to write: pick the days and a time, and NotTyped will remind you to pick up your pen.
    • Invite a friend from your profile or Settings.

  5. 1.4.0

    Oct 31, 2026
    • You decide what stays on your notes: remove any comment under them, and block or mute someone right from their comment.
    • Private notes now show a small lock, so you can tell at a glance which notes only you can see.
    • Activity is clearer: opening it clears the badge, replies say they're replies, and tapping a notification takes you straight to the comment or the person.

  6. 1.3.0

    Oct 23, 2026
    • Fix any word: hold a word to rewrite it, add a word before it, or delete it. No need to undo everything after it.
    • Undo now steps back one thing at a time, even a deleted word.
    • Gentle haptics when you confirm a word, like a note, and post.

  7. 1.2.0

    Oct 17, 2026
    • Watch any note being written: open a note and tap play to see it come to life stroke by stroke, with the sound of the pen.
    • Sharper notes on iPad and some older iPhones.
    • Steadier video exports, even for long notes.

  8. 1.1.3

    Oct 2, 2026

    A few quiet improvements under the paper. Same pen, smoother pages.

  9. 1.1.2

    Sep 25, 2026
    • Sharing got a new look.
    • Videos now sound like real writing.
    • Plus fixes and improvements.

  10. 1.1.1

    Sep 19, 2026
    • Share your notes as videos.
    • Plus fixes and improvements to make NotTyped more reliable.

  11. 1.1.0

    Sep 17, 2026

    Share as video was meant for this version and came in 1.1.1, two days later.

  12. 1.0.2

    Aug 25, 2026

    Small fixes. Back to writing.

  13. 1.0.1

    Aug 5, 2026

    Turn your phone sideways. Writing in landscape now gets the room it deserves: your note up top, the canvas below, nothing overlapping.

  14. 1.0

    Aug 4, 2026

    NotTyped arrives on the App Store.
