Why I built NotTyped

I built NotTyped because I got tired of scrolling through things no one actually wrote.

My feed was full of AI-generated images, AI-written captions, AI everything. I'd spend an hour scrolling and come away feeling like none of it was real. None of it was made by a person who sat down and thought about what they wanted to say.

Then I thought: what if there was a place where you had to actually write? Not type. Write. With your hand.

It turns out that when you slow down and put pen to paper, something changes. You think more carefully. You say what you mean. And your handwriting, messy, beautiful, completely yours, becomes your voice.

I also just love seeing other people's handwriting. Everyone's is different. Some people write tiny and neat. Some people write huge and chaotic. Every note tells you something about the person behind it before you even read the words.

NotTyped is a place to slow down, write something real, and see the real handwriting of real people. I hope you enjoy it.

David

What I do

I design, build, and run NotTyped: the app for iPhone and iPad, its backend, and this website. The pencil you hear as you write, and when a note writes itself, is a recording of my own pencil. NotTyped came out on August 4, 2026, and every release since is in the version history.
